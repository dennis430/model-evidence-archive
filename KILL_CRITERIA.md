# Kill Criteria for Archive

Separate from analytical core.

Gate A: Prove real provider calls occurred, records captured faithfully, failure states retained, inclusion/timestamp receipts verify where claimed. Fake samples or silent rewriting fatal.

Gate B: Instrument detects designed positive/negative controls, retains genuine baseline, distinguishes collection errors from behavioral outcomes.

Gate C: External reproduction or research use. Stable outputs not failure. Uninformative probes or indefensible claims are.

A correct historical archive can remain valuable as static record even without immediate citations.

States as in companion project. Active collection freezes at Day 30/90 per criteria.
